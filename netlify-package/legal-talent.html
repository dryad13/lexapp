Why firms use this

Hiring a junior takes months and a real commitment. This doesn't.

  • No recruitment cycleSkip the job ad, the agency fee, and the weeks of interviewing — start with someone pre-vetted within days.
  • Scale with your workloadBusy conveyancing quarter? Take on more hours. Quiet spell? Step back down. A permanent hire doesn't flex either way.
  • Genuinely outsourcedYour assistant is employed and managed by LexAssist throughout — we handle workload, performance and quality control. The legal work they support stays under your firm's professional supervision, exactly as it would with a paralegal you'd hired directly — we're not a supplier of temp labour working under your day-to-day direction.
  • Built for your SRA obligationsOutsourcing under the SRA Standards and Regulations calls for a written agreement, clear confidentiality protections, and a provider the SRA can access if it needs to. We're UK-based and structured to satisfy that from day one — not something you have to bolt on yourself.
  • One monthly invoiceNational Living Wage, employer National Insurance, pension contributions and holiday pay are all built into what we pay your assistant. Nothing extra lands on your payroll.
