The duties

What holding a licence actually commits you to

  • Report changes fastMost changes to a sponsored worker's role, salary or hours must be reported to UKVI within 10 working days. A worker leaving must be reported within 20.
  • Pay-period complianceFrom 8 April 2026, the full CoS salary must be paid in every pay period — not averaged out over the year.
  • Right-to-work checksDigital checks via eVisa and share codes are now mandatory, including follow-up re-checks before status expires.
  • Record keepingMigrant documents, contact details and employment records retained and ready to produce on request.
  • Cooperate with visitsUnannounced compliance visits are part of holding a licence — not an exception.

The evidence vault

When UKVI asks a sponsor for further information on a CoS, it asks the same categories of question, every time. The vault is built around exactly those categories, kept current instead of assembled under deadline pressure.

01

Genuine vacancy evidence

Why the role exists, how it differs from what the business already does, and evidence of the need — new contracts, expansion, a departure to replace, or market research.

02

Ability to pay

Business bank statements, payroll and RTI reports, and confirmation of how salaries are paid — ready for the "last six months" window UKVI typically asks for.

03

Organisational structure

A current hierarchy chart with every position, vacant roles, and where each sponsored worker sits — not redrawn from memory when asked.

04

Right to work & contracts

Signed employment contracts under the relevant SOC code, or a draft where you haven't sponsored that role before, alongside right-to-work evidence per worker.
