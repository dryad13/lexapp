The application, honestly

What the Home Office actually wants to see

This isn't a generic list — it's built from a real sponsor licence application, field for field.

  • Why you're applyingA clear explanation of why the business needs to sponsor, and which sector you operate in.
  • Business basicsCompany website, operating hours, and a full description of what the business does.
  • Hierarchy chartEvery position in the business, from owners and directors down, including vacant roles you intend to fill with sponsored workers.
  • The role itselfJob title, SOC code, duties, hours per week, minimum salary, and where it sits on your hierarchy chart.
  • Skills matchWhat skills, experience and qualifications the role requires — and evidence of how the person you have in mind meets them.
  • Track recordWhether you've held a licence before, whether any migrants are moving under TUPE, and details of any branch locations.
  • Key personnelA confirmed, secure email address for your Authorising Officer — accessible only to them.
