Where do you stand for the Skilled Worker route?

A starting point before you spend money on anything - check your salary against the current thresholds, see what else the route requires, and find out whether it's worth talking to someone.

Step one

Salary threshold checker

The Skilled Worker route runs on two salary tests, and you need to clear whichever is higher for your occupation.

This checks your salary against the general threshold (£41,700) or the new entrant rate (£33,400) only. Your actual occupation may carry a higher "going rate" under Appendix Skilled Occupations - the higher of the two always applies, and this tool doesn't know your SOC code yet. Treat this as a first filter, not a final answer.
