Why now

The rules changed a lot in the last year. They're not done.

  • Skill threshold raisedSponsored roles must now sit at RQF level 6 (graduate level) - around 180 occupations dropped off the eligible list from July 2025.
  • Salary thresholds upGeneral threshold now £41,700, new entrant rate £33,400, following the 2025 reforms.
  • Pay-period complianceFrom 8 April 2026, sponsors must pay the full CoS salary in every pay period - UKVI no longer waits for a year's data before acting.
  • Digital right to workeVisas and share-code checks are now mandatory - the old paper document checks no longer satisfy your duties on their own.
