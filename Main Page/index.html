Our Services

Immigration Application Support

We assist individuals and families with preparing and organising UK immigration applications. Our services include eligibility assessments, document preparation guidance, and structured application support to help ensure submissions are clear, accurate, and well-prepared for Home Office review.

Global Mobility & Business Immigration

LexAssist supports businesses expanding to the UK by advising on workforce relocation and corporate immigration pathways. We provide guidance on sponsor licences, Skilled Worker visas, and international hiring strategies to help companies move talent to the UK efficiently and compliantly.

Case Preparation

Some immigration matters involve unusual circumstances or complex histories. LexAssist helps clients prepare detailed documentation and structured evidence to support applications involving long residence, discretionary cases, or situations requiring careful case presentation.

Solicitor Support Services

LexAssist provides structured operational support to law firms, enhancing efficiency across onboarding and post-completion workflows. Our services are designed to reduce administrative burden, improve turnaround times, and maintain compliance with regulatory requirements.
